Things To Do in the Area

Between games, there's plenty to explore around Orange County and Southeast Texas. Local favorites — restaurants, attractions, and family activities — will be featured here.

Orange, TX

Shangri La Botanical Gardens

  • 252 acres of gardens, boardwalks, and wildlife along Adams Bayou — free admission.
  • 2111 W. Park Ave, Orange, TX
  • shangrilagardens.org →
Orange, TX

Stark Museum of Art & W.H. Stark House

  • American Western art collection plus a historic home tour in downtown Orange.
  • starkmuseum.org →
Beaumont, TX

Gator Country Adventure Park

  • Get up close with alligators and other rescued reptiles — a family favorite.
  • Get directions →
Beaumont, TX

Fire Museum of Texas & Downtown Murals

  • Home to the giant Dalmatian-spotted fire hydrant, plus Beaumont's public art and mural trail.
  • Beaumont CVB guide →
Beaumont, TX

Tyrrell Park: Cattail Marsh & Botanical Gardens

  • Boardwalk birdwatching over 900 acres of wetlands, plus 23 acres of gardens and a conservatory.
Port Arthur, TX

Museum of the Gulf Coast

  • Exhibits on Gulf Coast history and culture, including Janis Joplin and the Music Hall of Fame.
  • Visit Port Arthur guide →
Port Arthur, TX

Pleasure Island & Sea Rim State Park

  • Fishing, birding, a pirate ship playground, and a two-mile natural Gulf beach at Sea Rim.
Lake Charles, LA

Port Wonder — Children's Museum & Nature Center

  • Hands-on exhibits, freshwater/saltwater aquariums, and a nature and science center on the lakefront.
  • Visit Lake Charles guide →
Lake Charles, LA

Creole Nature Trail All-American Road

  • Scenic drive through marshlands to Gulf beaches — alligators and birdlife along the way.
Sulphur, LA

Almosta Ranch & SPAR Waterpark

  • Petting zoo and horse-drawn carriage rides at Almosta Ranch; splash pad and waterslides at SPAR Park.
Orange, TX

Airboat Rides — Sabine River / Blue Elbow Swamp

  • Guided airboat tours through the swamp and river — alligators, birds, and wildlife up close. Try Dragon Lady Airboats or Swamp & River Tours.
  • Dragon Lady Airboats →
Vidor, TX

Claiborne West Park

  • 453-acre wildlife and bird sanctuary with walking trails, disc golf, fishing pond, and playgrounds.
  • 4105 North St, Vidor, TX 77662
Orange, TX

W.H. Stark House

  • Historic 1894 home tour showcasing turn-of-the-century Orange — one of the four Stark Cultural Venues downtown.
Orange, TX

Free State Cellars

  • Local winery and vineyard with tastings, sangria, and live music on weekends. 21+ for tastings.
  • 4702 Tejas Pkwy, Orange, TX 77632
  • freestatecellars.com →
Orange, TX

Sunset Grove Country Club

  • Historic 1923 Donald Ross-designed 18-hole golf course. Private club — call ahead for guest access.
  • 2900 W Sunset Dr, Orange, TX 77630 • (409) 883-9392
Orange, TX

Orangefield Cormier Museum

  • Free admission. A recreated 1920s oil boomtown with vintage storefronts, antique cars, and a roller rink — right next to Orangefield High School.
  • 9974 FM 105, Orange, TX 77630
  • Open 3rd Saturdays, 10am–2pm, or by reservation: (409) 293-7340
Orange, TX

Texas Travel Information Center

  • Free maps, brochures, and a boardwalk nature walk into the swamp — right off I-10.
  • 1708 I-10 W, Orange, TX 77632
Vinton, LA

Delta Downs Racetrack, Casino & Hotel

  • Live horse racing, slots, and dining. Must be 21+ for the casino floor.
  • 2717 Delta Downs Dr, Vinton, LA 70668
Lake Charles, LA

Golden Nugget Lake Charles

  • Waterfront casino resort with a private beach, lazy river, and golf course. Must be 21+ for the casino floor.
  • 2550 Golden Nugget Blvd, Lake Charles, LA 70601
Lake Charles, LA

L'Auberge Casino Resort

  • Casino resort with a lazy river, golf course, and live entertainment. Must be 21+ for the casino floor.
  • 777 Avenue L'Auberge, Lake Charles, LA 70601
